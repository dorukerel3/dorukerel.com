NASA Kennedy Uzay Merkezi ile Bilkent Yaz Okulu gibi STEM kamplarına katılarak kendimi her alanda geliştirmeye devam ediyorum. I am a science high school student in Ankara. I develop end-to-end projects in hardware design, embedded systems software, and network management. I independently manage the entire hardware-software stack, from custom PCB design and 3D printing to VPN infrastructure and cybersecurity prototypes. Besides engineering, I compete in chemistry and mathematics olympiads (URFODU Chemistry, Waterloo Avogadro, Kanguru Mathematics). I received the College Board AP Scholar award (Calculus AB 5, Physics 1 5). Outside of my academic studies, I play piano and guitar, and create enjoyable rhythms with Cubase LE 15. I have earned a Cambridge English certificate. I actively participate in sports such as basketball, tennis, gymnastics, diving, and skiing. I continue to improve myself in all areas by participating in STEM camps such as the NASA Kennedy Space Center and the Bilkent Summer School.
